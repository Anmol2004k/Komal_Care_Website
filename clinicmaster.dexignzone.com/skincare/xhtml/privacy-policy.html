Section 01

Information We Collect

When you create an account, place an order, or interact with our store, we collect personal data that is strictly necessary to serve you well. Here is precisely what that includes:

  • Identity Information — Your full name and display name used during checkout or account creation.
  • Contact Details — Email address, phone number, and shipping/billing address.
  • Payment Information — Card details and transaction records processed securely through our payment gateway. We do not store raw card numbers on our servers.
  • Order History — Products purchased, quantities, dates, and delivery status.
  • Account Preferences — Saved addresses, wishlist items, and communication preferences.
  • Device & Technical Data — IP address, browser type, operating system, and referring URLs collected automatically when you visit our site.

We collect only what we genuinely need. We do not collect sensitive personal data such as national ID numbers, biometric data, or health records.

Section 02

Flavor & Scent Preferences

Our store is built around personalisation. When you browse, select, or rate products by flavour or scent (for example, choosing "citrus burst" hand cleanser over "oud & sandalwood"), we remember those choices.

Why we store this: Your preference data is used exclusively to surface relevant recommendations, personalise your homepage, and improve future product suggestions. It is never sold to advertisers or used for profiling outside our platform.

The data we store includes:

  • Flavour/scent categories you have clicked on or shortlisted
  • Products you have rated, reviewed, or added to a wishlist
  • Filters and sorting preferences applied during browsing sessions

You can reset or delete your flavour profile at any time from your Account → Preferences page.

Section 03

Screen Vibes & Usage Data

Our website features an interactive Screen Vibes experience — ambient animations and haptic-style feedback that respond to how you engage with the page. To deliver this, we collect limited usage data.

What is tracked: Scroll depth, click patterns, time spent on product pages, and — with your device's permission — haptic feedback responses if your browser supports the Vibration API. This data helps us calibrate the visual and tactile experience to your device's capabilities.

Usage data collected through Screen Vibes includes:

  • Interaction events (clicks, taps, hovers) used for animation triggers
  • Device performance signals (screen resolution, refresh rate, motion-preference settings)
  • Session duration and page engagement metrics

If you prefer a reduced-motion experience, enable "Reduce Motion" in your OS accessibility settings — our site respects the prefers-reduced-motion media query and will disable all kinetic effects automatically.

Section 04

Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. However, to operate our store, we share limited data with trusted service providers under strict contractual obligations. These partners are:

  • Delivery & Logistics Partners — Your name, delivery address, and order details are shared with our courier and fulfilment partners solely to ship your order. They are prohibited from using this data for any other purpose.
  • Payment Gateways — We use PCI-DSS compliant payment processors. Your payment data is transmitted directly to them via encrypted channels; we never handle raw card numbers.
  • Analytics Providers — Anonymised, aggregated usage data may be shared with analytics tools to help us understand site performance. This data cannot be used to identify you individually.
  • Customer Support Tools — If you raise a support ticket, relevant account details may be accessed through our helpdesk platform.
We require all third parties to maintain confidentiality, apply adequate security measures, and process your data only for specified, legitimate purposes consistent with this policy.

Section 06

Data Security

Protecting your data is a core responsibility. We employ the following technical and organisational measures:

  • SSL/TLS Encryption — All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using industry-standard TLS 1.2/1.3 protocols. Look for the padlock icon in your browser's address bar.
  • Payment Security — Card transactions are handled by PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment gateways. We tokenise payment methods so card details are never stored on our infrastructure.
  • Access Controls — Internal access to personal data is restricted on a strict need-to-know basis with role-based permissions and audit logging.
  • Data Minimisation — We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by applicable law.
  • Breach Response — In the event of a data breach that poses risk to your rights, we will notify you and relevant supervisory authorities within the timeframes required by law.
While we take every reasonable precaution, no online transmission is 100% secure. Please use a strong, unique password for your account and contact us immediately if you suspect any unauthorised access.

Section 07

Cookies

We use cookies — small text files stored in your browser — to make our site work properly and to improve your experience. Here is exactly what we use them for:

  • Essential Cookies — Required for the site to function. These remember your shopping cart (including that hand cleanser you added!), keep you logged in during a session, and enable checkout security. These cannot be disabled.
  • Preference Cookies — Store your language, region, and flavour preference settings so we can personalise your next visit without starting from scratch.
  • Analytics Cookies — Help us understand how visitors navigate the site (which pages they visit, where they drop off) so we can improve your experience. Data is aggregated and anonymised.
  • Marketing Cookies — Used only with your explicit consent to deliver relevant ads on partner platforms. You can withdraw consent at any time.

Cookie Categories & Retention:

Essential · Session Preference · 1 Year Analytics · 13 Months Marketing · 90 Days

You can manage or withdraw cookie consent at any time through our Cookie Settings banner (accessible via the footer link) or by adjusting your browser's privacy settings.
