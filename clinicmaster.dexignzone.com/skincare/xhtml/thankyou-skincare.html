Morning Routine

Gentle cleanser → Toner → Vitamin C Serum → Moisturizer → SPF 50. These 5 steps will shield your skin beautifully throughout the day.

Night Ritual

Always double cleanse at night. Apply a retinol or peptide serum before bed — skin repairs and regenerates while you sleep.

Just 5 minutes of daily facial massage boosts blood circulation and lymphatic drainage. Use a gua sha or fingers with upward strokes.

Very hot water strips your skin's natural oils and causes dryness. Use lukewarm water and always pat dry — never rub.
